# Report delivery fee gate

School Administrators can configure whether approved report cards are withheld while a learner has an outstanding fee balance.

When enabled:

- the release process calculates the remaining balance after discounts and non-voided payments;
- learners with an outstanding balance are skipped during report release;
- skipped reports remain unreleased;
- guardian report-release notifications are not sent for those learners;
- academic scores and promotion decisions are unaffected;
- an approved report intentionally withheld for fees still counts as ready for academic-year rollover, so fee debt does not block promotion processing.

The setting is available in **School Setup → Report Delivery**.
